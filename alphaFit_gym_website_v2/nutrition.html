Nutrition Support

Fuel Your Training with Balanced Meals That Feel Realistic and Easy to Sustain.

Nutrition at AlphaFit is about better habits, steadier energy, and food choices that support the way you train.

Nutrition Philosophy

Good Food Makes Training Feel Easier

When meals are balanced, workouts feel more stable and recovery becomes smoother.

The goal is not extreme dieting. The goal is to build sustainable habits: enough protein, steady hydration, and meals that support energy across the day.

AlphaFit's nutrition approach is designed around the Indian lifestyle — whole foods, practical portioning, and meals that fit a busy schedule without requiring hours of meal prep.

Core Rules

Simple Rules Members Follow Every Week

  • Drink 2.5 to 3 litres of water throughout the day
  • Include protein in each main meal
  • Add vegetables and fibre to at least two meals
  • Keep portions realistic and consistent
  • Eat before and after training sessions
Macronutrients

Understanding What Your Body Needs

Balanced nutrition is not about eliminating food groups. It is about getting the right amount of each one.

Protein

Build and Repair Muscle

Protein is the foundation of every fitness goal. Whether building muscle or losing fat, adequate protein protects your body composition.

1.6
g per kg

Minimum daily target for active members

2.2
g per kg

Target for muscle gain phases

Sources: paneer, chicken, dal, eggs, Greek yoghurt, tofu, whey protein.

Carbohydrates

Fuel Every Session

Carbohydrates are the body's primary fuel source. Timing them around training helps performance and recovery.

  • Eat complex carbs 1–2 hours before training
  • Include carbs in your post-workout meal
  • Choose whole grains, rice, oats, fruits
  • Reduce refined sugar and processed foods
Fats

Support Hormones and Recovery

Healthy fats support hormone production, joint health, and long-lasting energy. They should not be removed from the diet.

  • Include nuts and seeds daily
  • Use ghee, olive oil, or coconut oil in moderation
  • Eat fatty fish or flaxseed for omega-3s
  • Avoid trans fats and heavily processed oils
Meal Ideas

Simple Ideas for a Stronger Daily Routine

Every meal idea below is designed around practical Indian food and a realistic lifestyle.

Breakfast

Oats, Fruit, and Protein

Start the day with something light, filling, and easy to digest before work or class.

  • Oats with milk and banana
  • Boiled eggs or paneer bhurji
  • Idli with sambar and chutney
  • Upma with vegetables
Post-Workout

Protein and Recovery

Use a balanced meal or shake after training to support muscle repair and energy refilling.

  • Whey protein shake with a banana
  • Paneer or chicken with rice
  • Curd rice with vegetables
  • Dal and roti with salad
Dinner

Balanced Plate

Keep dinner simple with protein, vegetables, and a moderate portion of complex carbs.

  • Grilled chicken or fish with vegetables
  • Dal, sabzi, and one roti
  • Khichdi with curd
  • Egg curry with brown rice
Sample Day

Example Meal Schedule

  • Morning: oats with fruit and a protein source
  • Pre-workout: banana and a handful of nuts
  • Post-workout: whey or paneer with a light meal
  • Lunch: rice or roti with dal, vegetables, and lean protein
  • Evening: fruit, nuts, or yoghurt
  • Dinner: balanced plate with protein, vegetables, and complex carbs
Nutrition Tips

Easy Advice for Members

Drink Enough Water

Hydration matters before, during, and after training. Start with a glass before every meal.

Eat on Time

Regular meals support energy and workout consistency. Skipping meals leads to overeating later.

Keep Balance

Healthy food habits matter more than extreme diets. Progress happens through consistency, not perfection.
